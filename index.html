When India Was Tested

In every war and every operation, India's soldiers answered the call with unimaginable valour.

1999
⚔️
Kargil War
May — July 1999 · Operation Vijay
Pakistani infiltrators occupied Indian peaks at 15,000–18,000 ft. The Indian Army fought brutal uphill battles in sub-zero conditions to reclaim every inch of sovereign territory.
527+Martyrs
1,363Wounded
Peak 5140 captured at dawn, Point 4875 reclaimed by Vikram Batra — India's flag flew again over every peak by July 26.
1971
🎖️
Bangladesh Liberation War
December 1971 · Operation Trident
India's finest military hour — a swift 13-day campaign that liberated Bangladesh, led by Field Marshal Sam Manekshaw. The largest military surrender since World War II followed.
3,843Martyrs
93,000POWs Taken
Pakistan's Eastern Command surrendered on December 16, 1971 — now celebrated as Vijay Diwas.
1962
🏔️
Sino-Indian War
October — November 1962
Against overwhelming odds and inadequate supplies, Indian soldiers fought heroic delaying actions in the Himalayas. The Battle of Rezang La saw 114 men fight over 1,000 — every bullet spent.
3,000+Martyrs
114Heroes of Rezang La
When the snow melted in spring 1963, every man of C Company was found dead, still facing the enemy.
1984
🪖
Operation Meghdoot
April 1984 · Siachen Glacier
India's audacious pre-dawn airlift of troops onto the world's highest battlefield — the Siachen Glacier at 22,000 ft. Soldiers continue to guard this icy post at the cost of their lives, year after year.
22,000 ftAltitude
40 yearsOf vigil
More Indian soldiers have died from the extreme cold and altitude than from enemy fire — yet no post has ever been abandoned.
1965
🛡️
Indo-Pakistan War 1965
August — September 1965 · Operation Grand Slam
Pakistan launched a massive armoured thrust in Operation Grand Slam. India's response — Operation Riddle — led to the Battle of Asal Uttar, where Pakistan's vaunted Patton tanks were turned to burning wrecks.
3,264Martyrs
97Patton Tanks Destroyed
Pakistan's 1 Armoured Division — the "Sword Arm" — was shattered at Asal Uttar. Havildar Abdul Hamid destroyed seven tanks alone.
2008
🦅

November 26–29, 2008 · NSG Operation
Ten terrorists paralysed India's financial capital for three nights. The NSG's Black Cats, Mumbai Police, and MARCOS commandos fought building by building, floor by floor, to neutralise every threat.

Military History — इतिहास

India's Timeline of Valour

From Independence to the present — a nation's unbroken story of sacrifice and strength.

1947
Independence & First Kashmir War
India gains freedom. The very next day, Pakistan-backed tribal forces invade Kashmir. Indian Army airlifted to Srinagar — the first test of the new nation's armed forces.
1962
Sino-Indian War
China attacks across the Himalayas. Outnumbered Indian soldiers fight with frozen rifles and minimal supplies, showing extraordinary courage against overwhelming odds.
1965
Indo-Pakistan War
Pakistan's Operation Grand Slam meets India's iron resolve. The Battle of Asal Uttar shatters Pakistan's armoured corps. India fights to a honourable stalemate and stands unbroken.
1971
Bangladesh Liberation
India's finest military hour. A 13-day campaign liberates 75 million people. Sam Manekshaw's masterstroke ends with the largest military surrender since WWII — Vijay Diwas, December 16.
1984
Operation Meghdoot
India captures the Siachen Glacier at 22,000 ft — the world's highest battlefield. A bold pre-emptive move that India has held for four decades through unimaginable sacrifice.
1999
Kargil War
Pakistani infiltration triggers India's most televised war. Operation Vijay sees Indian soldiers storm impossible peaks in summer snow and under live fire. Every peak reclaimed. Vijay.
2016
Surgical Strikes
In retaliation for the Uri attack that killed 19 soldiers, India conducts precision surgical strikes across the LoC — a powerful demonstration of decisive force and military capability.
Today
Eternal Vigil
From Siachen to the Rann of Kutch, from the Eastern frontier to the deep seas — 1.4 million soldiers stand watch tonight, so every Indian can sleep safely. Their sacrifice continues.
